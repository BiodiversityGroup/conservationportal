Species and taxonomy databases

Names, threat status, distributions and the records behind them.

32 resources

  • GBIF↗

    Global occurrence records

    Download occurrence records for a species or region as the starting dataset for range and gap analysis

  • IUCN Red List↗

    Global threat assessments

    Establish what threat category a species carries, then read the assessment rationale rather than just the label

  • Catalogue of Life↗

    Names backbone

    Resolve a scientific name against the global taxonomic backbone before joining datasets built on different name sources

  • ITIS↗

    Taxonomic names, strongest for North America

    Match North American names to a stable federal taxonomy when reconciling a US agency dataset

  • Encyclopedia of Life↗

    Species pages and an education arm

    Pull ready-made species pages and trait data when building education material rather than writing from scratch

  • NatureServe Explorer↗

    Conservation status ranks including plants and invertebrates, North America

    Look up a subnational rank for plants and invertebrates the global Red List has never assessed

  • Species+↗

    CITES and CMS listings per species

    Confirm the current CITES appendix and CMS listing for a species before planning any cross-border shipment

  • Map of Life↗

    Range maps and indicators

    Overlay modeled ranges to see what should occur at a site you have not yet surveyed

  • Reptile Database↗

    Reptile taxonomy

    Check whether a reptile name is current before publishing, since the group is split and renamed constantly

  • Amphibian Species of the World↗

    Amphibian taxonomy

    Settle amphibian nomenclature against the reference most herpetological journals expect authors to follow

  • AmphibiaWeb↗

    Amphibian species accounts

    Read a species account and the current global amphibian count when scoping a project or proposal

  • Amphibian Conservation Needs Assessments↗

    Per-species conservation needs

    See which specific actions experts think a species needs, which is more actionable than a threat category

  • Mammal Diversity Database↗

    Mammal taxonomy

    Track mammal taxonomic changes so a species list built last year still matches what reviewers expect

  • All The World's Primates↗

    Primate species accounts

    Look up a primate species account when scoping surveys in a region you do not know

  • Elephant Database↗

    Elephant records

    Look up historic captive and wild elephant records when reconstructing a population history

  • Rhino Resource Center↗

    Rhino literature and images

    Search decades of rhino literature and imagery, including old reports that never reached the journals

  • AnimalBase↗

    Early zoological names and types

    Trace an early zoological name to its original description and type material when resolving a nomenclatural problem

  • Avian Knowledge Network↗

    Bird data network

    Access pooled US bird monitoring data across agencies instead of negotiating with each one separately

  • FishBase↗

    Fish species data

    Pull life history traits, length weight relationships and diet data for stock and vulnerability work

  • Shark-Base↗

    Elasmobranch records

    Check elasmobranch records when compiling a regional shark and ray species list

  • OBIS↗

    Marine occurrences

    Download marine occurrence data for a sea area, then check publisher country to judge sampling bias

  • WoRMS↗

    Marine species register

    Validate marine species names against the accepted register before submitting occurrence data anywhere

  • GlobalFungi↗

    Fungal occurrence database

    Query soil fungal sequence occurrences when asking whether a habitat's fungal community is documented at all

  • SEINet / Symbiota↗

    Herbarium networks with a checklist generator

    Generate a herbarium-backed plant checklist for a site, with specimen records standing behind every name

  • Calflora↗

    California plant occurrences and rarity

    Map documented occurrences of a rare California plant before ground-truthing a proposed development site

  • Jepson eFlora↗

    California flora

    Key out a California plant and confirm which name the state's floristic standard accepts

  • antmaps.org↗

    Ant distributions

    Compare ant species lists between two regions to see which introductions are already established

  • Recently Extinct Plants and Animals Database↗

    Extinctions including plants and invertebrates

    Check whether a species is already treated as extinct, including plants and invertebrates most lists skip

  • World Database of Key Biodiversity Areas↗

    KBA boundaries and triggers

    Check whether your project area sits inside a Key Biodiversity Area and which species triggered it

  • Alliance for Zero Extinction↗

    AZE sites and species

    Find out if a site holds the only population of a threatened species, which reframes the funding case

  • Local Biodiversity Outlooks↗

    Community biodiversity reporting

    Cite community-authored case studies when arguing that local and Indigenous action delivers the global targets

  • snakeDB↗

    Referenced venom and LD50 data for snake research

    Look up venom and LD50 figures with a source attached, rather than numbers that circulate without one
