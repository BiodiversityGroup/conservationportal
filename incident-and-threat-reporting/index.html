Incident and threat reporting

Where to report a problem rather than a sighting: roadkill, strandings, poisoning, invasives and lost fishing gear.

27 resources
  • California Roadkill Observation System

    Submit geotagged roadkill photos anywhere in California, from the UC Davis Road Ecology Center, whose same site indexes roadkill programs worldwide

  • The Road Lab

    UK national roadkill reporting by app, form or social media, past 103,000 reports. Renamed from Project Splatter in 2022, so search both

  • Projekt Roadkill

    Austria's roadkill reporting through the SPOTTERON app or a web form, run by BOKU Vienna

  • Taiwan Roadkill Observation Network

    Taiwan's national roadkill reporting. The site blocks automated tools but works in a browser, and its data was still flowing in mid-2026

  • Srazena zver

    Czech animal-vehicle collision reporting by hunters and the public, run by the Transport Research Centre. Registration needed

  • EWT Wildlife and Transport Programme

    South Africa's roadkill database and RoadWatch app, around 30,000 reports. Note the domain moved from ewt.org.za

  • Sistema Urubu

    Brazil's app for reporting animals killed on highways and railways. Nothing on the site is newer than 2022, so check the app before relying on it

  • MISIN

    Early-detection invasive species reporting and identification training for the US Midwest, from Michigan State

  • iMapInvasives

    NatureServe's invasive reporting network, currently covering Arizona, Maine, New York, Oregon and Pennsylvania plus Canadian partners

  • EASIN

    The EU's invasive alien species information system, with a citizen reporting app and the official Member State notification route

  • Invading Species Awareness Program

    Ontario's invasive species hotline and volunteer surveillance, including wild pig reporting

  • USGS Nonindigenous Aquatic Species

    Report an aquatic invasive sighting in the US and subscribe to its alert system

  • FeralScan

    Australia's pest animal reporting, and through WomSAT it also takes wombat mange and dead-wombat records

  • GB non-native species alerts

    Britain's rapid reporting route for alert species, including Asian hornet. The old asianhornet URL now points here

  • RSPB: report crimes against wild birds

    How to report raptor persecution, poisoning and nest destruction in the UK

  • African Wildlife Poisoning Database

    Report a poisoning incident anywhere in Africa, run by the IUCN Vulture Specialist Group with EWT. The form works, though the public statistics have not moved since 2022

  • End Wildlife Trafficking Online: report a listing

    Report suspected illegal wildlife products for sale online. The form states it does not currently accept reports from Europe

  • dBird

    Report a bird killed or injured by a collision, with over 38,000 records from 119 organizations

  • Global Bird Collision Mapper

    Worldwide reporting of bird collisions with buildings, run by FLAP Canada

  • Garden Wildlife Health

    Report a sick or dead garden bird, amphibian, hedgehog or reptile in the UK, and it goes to a vet pathology network

  • Canadian Wildlife Health Cooperative

    Report sick or dead wildlife in Canada, and submit carcasses for disease diagnosis

  • UK Cetacean Strandings Investigation Programme

    Report a stranded cetacean, marine turtle or basking shark in the UK. Running since 1990

  • NOAA: report a stranded or injured marine animal

    US reporting for stranded, entangled, injured or dead marine mammals and sea turtles, with regional hotlines

  • ORRCA

    24-hour marine mammal and turtle rescue hotline plus incident reporting for New South Wales

  • COASST

    Beached bird and marine mortality surveys on the northeast Pacific coast. Volunteers are trained rather than casual reporters

  • Global Ghost Gear Initiative data portal

    Report and browse records of lost and abandoned fishing gear. Note the working domain is ghostgear.org

  • Marine Debris Tracker

    Log plastic and debris pollution anywhere in the world from your phone
