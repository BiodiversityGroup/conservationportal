Citizen science recording projects

Individual schemes you send records to, by taxon and by country. Most want a photograph and five minutes.

  • HerpMapper↗

    Herp occurrence reporting

    Recording project

    Submit reptile and amphibian records that reach state agencies, with locality obscuring for sensitive species

  • FrogID↗

    Australian frog call project

    Recording projectAustralia

    Record a frog call on a phone and get a verified record into the national dataset

  • JellyWatch↗

    Marine invertebrate sightings

    Recording project

    Report gelatinous plankton sightings, but confirm the site is reachable before building a survey on it

  • NANPA citizen science↗

    Photography-based citizen science

    Recording project

    Fold photography into a recording project, and reach photographers who could document your species

  • Global Sawfish Encounters↗

    Sawfish sighting map

    Recording project

    Report a sawfish encounter, and mine the sighting map when scoping surveys in remaining habitat

  • Online Jaguar Observation Database↗

    Jaguar sightings

    Recording project

    Filter jaguar observations by evidence type, which matters when a record is a track rather than a photograph

  • Latest Sightings↗

    Safari sighting app

    Recording project

    Watch safari sighting reports as an informal signal of where large mammals are being seen

  • Seabirds.net↗

    Seabird colony registry

    Recording project

    Check colony registry records, but verify the host is reachable before you depend on it

  • Bumble Bee Watch↗

    Photograph a bumblebee anywhere in the US or Canada and an expert verifies the species. Running at over 16,000 sightings a month

    Recording projectUS

    Get a bumblebee photograph verified by an expert, which few insect schemes manage at this volume

  • European Butterfly Monitoring Scheme↗

    Pan-European butterfly and moth transect counts across 6,000+ transects, with a recording app in ten or more languages

    Recording projectEurope

    Adopt its transect protocol so your counts stay comparable with thousands of European sites

  • Tagfalter-Monitoring Deutschland↗

    Germany's weekly volunteer butterfly transect walks, running since 2005. In German

    Recording projectEurope

    Borrow a weekly transect design tested over twenty years, if you can read German

  • Catalan Butterfly Monitoring Scheme↗

    Butterfly transects across Catalonia, the Balearics and Andorra since 1994. In Catalan

    Recording projectEurope

    Use one of the longest butterfly time series in Europe as a Mediterranean reference

  • Correo Real↗

    Mexico's monarch migration monitoring network, run by Profauna since 1992. Report by form, WhatsApp or email. In Spanish

    Recording projectLatin America

    Report monarchs moving through Mexico by WhatsApp, form or email, which lowers the barrier for rural observers

  • Butterflies of India↗

    Peer-reviewed national butterfly database for India, built largely from public photographs

    Recording projectAsia

    Check a peer-reviewed Indian record before treating a butterfly photograph as a range extension

  • Mosquito Alert↗

    Photo-report invasive mosquitoes, bites and breeding sites. Spanish in origin, now international. Spanish, Catalan and English

    Recording projectGlobal

    Report invasive mosquitoes and breeding sites, and see how a vector scheme handles several languages

  • Mueckenatlas↗

    Germany asks you to post the actual mosquito in, for identification and mapping. Over 42,000 specimens sent in. In German

    Recording projectEurope

    Study a scheme that collects physical specimens by post, for when photographs cannot settle identification

  • Riverfly Monitoring Initiative↗

    UK anglers and volunteers kick-sample mayflies, stoneflies and caddisflies as an early warning on water quality

    Recording projectUK

    Train volunteers to kick-sample invertebrates as an early warning on water quality between official tests

  • British Dragonfly Society recording scheme↗

    National dragonfly and damselfly records through county recorders and iRecord, UK

    Recording projectUK

    Submit dragonfly records through county recorders and iRecord, joining over two million verified observations

  • UK Glow Worm Survey↗

    Volunteer counts of glow-worms at known sites, feeding the Biological Records Centre

    Recording project

    Count glow-worms at a known site and add the record to the UK national dataset

  • FrogWatch USA↗

    Trained volunteers report frog and toad breeding calls from February to August across the US

    Recording projectUS

    Train volunteers to report breeding calls across a fixed February to August season

  • RAVON↗

    The Netherlands' volunteer recording networks for reptiles, amphibians and freshwater fish. In Dutch

    Recording projectEurope

    Copy a national volunteer structure for herps and freshwater fish, if Dutch is not a barrier

  • TurtleSAT↗

    Map Australian freshwater turtle sightings, nests and road deaths. Over 34,000 records and 2,000 nests protected

    Recording projectAustralia

    Map turtle nests and road deaths together, which is how you find where fencing would pay off

  • Great Eggcase Hunt↗

    Report shark, skate and ray eggcases found on the beach. UK-led with a newer Australian arm

    Recording projectUK

    Turn a beach walk into elasmobranch distribution data, and use it as a low-skill volunteer entry point

  • Big Seaweed Search↗

    Timed seaweed surveys on UK shores, run by the Natural History Museum with the Marine Conservation Society

    Recording projectUK

    Run a timed shore survey using a protocol a museum has already validated

  • National Plant Monitoring Scheme↗

    Survey indicator plants in a randomly allocated 1 km square, UK. Ten years of data as of 2025

    Recording projectUK

    Take a randomly allocated square so your plant data escapes the bias of picking attractive sites

  • Nature's Calendar↗

    UK phenology records for plants, animals and fungi, continuing a series that starts in 1736

    Recording projectUK

    Add phenology records to a series reaching back to 1736, which almost no climate dataset can match

  • SeasonWatch↗

    Track leaf flush, flowering and fruiting on 130+ common Indian tree species

    Recording projectAsia

    Recruit school students to track flowering and fruiting on trees they already walk past

  • Fungimap↗

    Australia's national fungal recording scheme, with nearly 5,000 observers on its annual hunt

    Recording projectAustralia

    Join or copy Australia's national fungal hunt, one of few schemes treating fungi as a recording target

  • Hedgehog Street↗

    Report UK hedgehog sightings and map the holes they move through between gardens

    Recording projectUK

    Map the holes animals move through between gardens, which is how a street becomes connected habitat

  • Vigie-Chiro↗

    France's volunteer bat acoustic monitoring, by fixed point, car transect or roost count, with automated call identification. In French

    Recording projectEurope

    Adopt a bat acoustic protocol with automated call identification already built in, if you read French

  • National Dormouse Monitoring Programme↗

    Volunteer nest-box monitoring for hazel dormouse across the UK

    Recording projectUK

    Report a dormouse sighting, or base your own nest-box monitoring on an established national database
