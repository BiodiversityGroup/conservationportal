Recording and data

Citizen science recording projects

Individual schemes you send records to, by taxon and by country. Most want a photograph and five minutes.

31 resources
  • HerpMapper

    Herp occurrence reporting

  • FrogID

    Australian frog call project

  • JellyWatch

    Marine invertebrate sightings

  • NANPA citizen science

    Photography-based citizen science

  • Global Sawfish Encounters

    Sawfish sighting map

  • Online Jaguar Observation Database

    Jaguar sightings

  • Latest Sightings

    Safari sighting app

  • Seabirds.net

    Seabird colony registry

  • Bumble Bee Watch

    Photograph a bumblebee anywhere in the US or Canada and an expert verifies the species. Running at over 16,000 sightings a month

  • European Butterfly Monitoring Scheme

    Pan-European butterfly and moth transect counts across 6,000+ transects, with a recording app in ten or more languages

  • Tagfalter-Monitoring Deutschland

    Germany's weekly volunteer butterfly transect walks, running since 2005. In German

  • Catalan Butterfly Monitoring Scheme

    Butterfly transects across Catalonia, the Balearics and Andorra since 1994. In Catalan

  • Correo Real

    Mexico's monarch migration monitoring network, run by Profauna since 1992. Report by form, WhatsApp or email. In Spanish

  • Butterflies of India

    Peer-reviewed national butterfly database for India, built largely from public photographs

  • Mosquito Alert

    Photo-report invasive mosquitoes, bites and breeding sites. Spanish in origin, now international. Spanish, Catalan and English

  • Mueckenatlas

    Germany asks you to post the actual mosquito in, for identification and mapping. Over 42,000 specimens sent in. In German

  • Riverfly Monitoring Initiative

    UK anglers and volunteers kick-sample mayflies, stoneflies and caddisflies as an early warning on water quality

  • British Dragonfly Society recording scheme

    National dragonfly and damselfly records through county recorders and iRecord, UK

  • UK Glow Worm Survey

    Volunteer counts of glow-worms at known sites, feeding the Biological Records Centre

  • FrogWatch USA

    Trained volunteers report frog and toad breeding calls from February to August across the US

  • RAVON

    The Netherlands' volunteer recording networks for reptiles, amphibians and freshwater fish. In Dutch

  • TurtleSAT

    Map Australian freshwater turtle sightings, nests and road deaths. Over 34,000 records and 2,000 nests protected

  • Great Eggcase Hunt

    Report shark, skate and ray eggcases found on the beach. UK-led with a newer Australian arm

  • Big Seaweed Search

    Timed seaweed surveys on UK shores, run by the Natural History Museum with the Marine Conservation Society

  • National Plant Monitoring Scheme

    Survey indicator plants in a randomly allocated 1 km square, UK. Ten years of data as of 2025

  • Nature's Calendar

    UK phenology records for plants, animals and fungi, continuing a series that starts in 1736

  • SeasonWatch

    Track leaf flush, flowering and fruiting on 130+ common Indian tree species

  • Fungimap

    Australia's national fungal recording scheme, with nearly 5,000 observers on its annual hunt

  • Hedgehog Street

    Report UK hedgehog sightings and map the holes they move through between gardens

  • Vigie-Chiro

    France's volunteer bat acoustic monitoring, by fixed point, car transect or roost count, with automated call identification. In French

  • National Dormouse Monitoring Programme

    Volunteer nest-box monitoring for hazel dormouse across the UK
