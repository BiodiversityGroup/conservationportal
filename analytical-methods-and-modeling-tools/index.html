Analytical methods and modeling tools

Software for viability, connectivity, occupancy, camera traps and acoustics.

19 resources
  • Vortex

    Free population viability analysis for extinction-risk simulation

  • Circuitscape

    Open-source circuit-theory tool for landscape connectivity and gene flow

  • Linkage Mapper

    ArcGIS toolbox that maps and prioritizes wildlife corridors

  • BOLD Systems

    DNA barcode database and analysis workbench for species identification

  • TDWG Biodiversity Information Standards

    The standards body for biodiversity data, and home of Darwin Core, the vocabulary everything from GBIF down uses to share occurrence and specimen records

  • eBio Atlas

    Open global eDNA biodiversity repository, freshwater focus

  • Maxent

    Maximum-entropy species distribution modeling software

  • Wallace EcoMod

    Guided R and Shiny platform for reproducible niche modeling

  • Program MARK

    Capture-recapture survival and population parameter estimation

  • rOpenSci

    Peer-reviewed hub for open-source R packages used in ecology

  • unmarked (R package)

    Fits occupancy, distance sampling and N-mixture abundance models to survey data where detection is imperfect

  • camtrapR (R package)

    Turns raw camera-trap images into detection histories, occupancy model inputs and activity patterns

  • MegaDetector

    Free AI model that finds animals, people and vehicles in camera-trap images so you never review a blank frame again

  • AddaxAI

    Runs MegaDetector and species classifiers through a desktop interface, with no coding required. Formerly EcoAssist

  • Timelapse2

    Free tool for reviewing and annotating large camera-trap image sets, and it imports MegaDetector results

  • Camelot

    Free camera-trap database and classification tool that exports straight to camtrapR and PRESENCE

  • Raven

    Cornell Lab sound analysis software for visualizing and measuring animal calls. Raven Lite is free, Raven Pro is paid

  • Kaleidoscope

    Sorts and labels bird song, frog calls and bat echolocation across months of recordings. Lite edition is free

  • bioacoustics (R package)

    Free GPL-3 package that filters noisy audio and extracts acoustic features for classification, reading WAV, WAC, MP3 and ZC
