Prizes, awards and competitions

What you can enter or be nominated for. Check which ones take an application, because many do not.

32 resources

/
All categoriesFundingSpecies dataField toolsMappingTraining
  • Whitley Awards↗

    £50,000 each to six conservation leaders working in their own country. You do not need to be nominated, you apply

    Award

    Apply directly with no nomination needed, which is unusual for an award of this size

  • Future For Nature Award↗

    €50,000 each to three young conservationists. Open application

    Award

    Apply if you are between 18 and 35, since the age band is the real filter

  • St Andrews Prize for the Environment↗

    $95,000 to the winner and $20,000 to each runner-up. Open to individuals, organizations and community groups

    Award

    Enter as a group rather than an individual, which most prizes at this level disallow

  • Rolex Awards↗

    Five laureates a year across environment, science and exploration. Open to anyone 18 or over, now annual rather than biennial

    Award

    Apply as an individual over 18, now yearly rather than every second year

  • Explorers Club grants↗

    Six named grant programs with live deadlines, from $1,500 to $10,000, plus rolling Impact Grants

    Award

    Match your project to one of six named programs, or use rolling Impact Grants when timing slips

  • WILDLABS Awards↗

    $360,000 across 16 conservation-tech projects in 2026. Anyone can submit the first-stage expression of interest

    Award

    Send a first-stage expression of interest, which anyone can do without an invitation

  • National Geographic Society grants and Wayfinder Award↗

    Explorer grants are the open-application route; the Wayfinder Award is by selection, not application

    Award

    Apply through the Explorer grants route, since the Wayfinder Award cannot be applied for

  • Goldman Environmental Prize↗

    $200,000 each to six grassroots activists, one per world region. Closed to unsolicited nominations

    Award

    Do not spend time here unless a nominating partner has already approached you

  • Indianapolis Prize↗

    $250,000 biennially, nomination only, but the Emerging Conservationist award for under-40s takes direct applications

    Award

    Aim at the Emerging Conservationist award if under 40, since the main prize is nomination only

  • Tyler Prize for Environmental Achievement↗

    $250,000, nomination only. The 2026 laureate won it for work on mycorrhizal fungi

    Award

    Nominate someone else rather than yourself, and note that nominations close well ahead

  • Blue Planet Prize↗

    Two laureates a year at 50 million yen each, roughly $340,000. Nominated by designated nominators

    Award

    Get a designated nominator to put you forward, which means building that relationship first

  • Earthshot Prize↗

    £1,000,000 to each of five winners, one being Protect and Restore Nature. Nominated through official nominator organizations, several of which run public expressions of interest

    Award

    Watch which nominator organizations run public calls, since that is the only open door

  • Tusk Conservation Awards↗

    £225,000 across three awards including a ranger award, paid to the winner's organization over three years. Africa only

    AwardAfrica

    Nominate an African colleague, a ranger included, with the money going to their organization

  • UNESCO Sultan Qaboos Prize↗

    $100,000 for environmental conservation. Nominated by governments or NGOs in official relations with UNESCO, no self-nomination

    Award

    Ask a government or a UNESCO-linked NGO to nominate you, because self-nomination is refused

  • MIDORI Prize for Biodiversity↗

    Biennial biodiversity prize run with the CBD Secretariat, and nominations come in through the website

    Award

    Submit through the website in a nomination year, run jointly with the CBD Secretariat

  • Seacology Prize↗

    $10,000 to an islander for preserving island environments and culture. The nomination route is not published, so ask them

    Award

    Contact them directly about nominating an islander, since the route is not published anywhere

  • Ashden Awards↗

    Up to £40,000, free to enter. Mostly clean energy, but the Tropical Forest Protection category is a real fit

    Award

    Enter the Tropical Forest Protection category free, which is the one that fits conservation work

  • Disney Conservation Hero Awards↗

    $1,000 per hero. Only nominable by nonprofits Disney already funds, so useful if you are one and not otherwise

    Award

    Skip it unless Disney already funds you, because nobody else is allowed to nominate

  • Xerces Deborah BanDrosky Award↗

    Student research award of about $5,000 for any declining invertebrate, from fireflies to freshwater mollusks. New in 2026, applications open each November

    Award

    Point a student at it each November, since almost nothing else funds declining invertebrates broadly

  • Xerces Joan Mosenthal DeWind Award↗

    Two student awards a year of about $5,000 for Lepidoptera conservation. Applications open each November

    Award

    Send Lepidoptera students here, where two awards a year makes the odds unusually good

  • Royal Entomological Society Insect Conservation Award↗

    £400 for insect and invertebrate conservation, and self-nomination is accepted. The live successor to the discontinued Marsh insect award

    Award

    Nominate yourself, which is rare, and treat it as successor to the old Marsh insect award

  • Behler Turtle Conservation Award↗

    $5,000, treated as the top award in turtle and tortoise conservation. Nominated by specialist group members

    Award

    Ask a specialist group member to nominate you, since that is the only route in

  • ASA Future Leaders of Amphibian Conservation↗

    Course fees, conference travel and project grants up to about $5,000 for early-career amphibian conservationists. Open application

    Award

    Apply for course fees and conference travel, which most grants flatly refuse to cover

  • Augustin-Pyramus de Candolle Prize↗

    CHF 5,000 for the best monographic treatment of a plant group, and fungi and lichens count. Has no permanent page, so the link moves each cycle

    Award

    Bookmark the current call rather than the page, because the link changes every cycle

  • Marsh Award for International Plant Conservation↗

    Plant conservation award run with Botanic Gardens Conservation International. Nominated through the BGCI network

    AwardGlobal

    Get nominated through the BGCI network, so work with a botanic garden well beforehand

  • Marsh Charitable Trust awards↗

    Thirty-plus awards, unusually dense in invertebrates and plants: entomology, four Lepidoptera awards, botany, marine and freshwater. No cash values published

    Award

    Scan all thirty for the invertebrate and plant awards, which are unusually well represented here

  • David Fairchild Medal for Plant Exploration↗

    $5,000 every three years for plant exploration and rare plant conservation, next in 2027. Same page carries two student scholarships

    Award

    Note the next cycle is 2027, and check the two student scholarships on the same page

  • GBIF Ebbe Nielsen Challenge↗

    €20,000 prize pool for tools built on GBIF-mediated data. Call opens early in the year, closes late June

    Award

    Enter if you already built a tool on GBIF data, since the work is done

  • Wildlife Photographer of the Year↗

    £10,000 to the grand title winner. Entry fee is waived for under-26s and for residents of fifty listed countries

    Award

    Check the fee waiver list first, since under-26s and fifty listed countries enter free

  • BigPicture: Natural World↗

    $5,000 grand prize plus category prizes, and exhibition at the California Academy of Sciences. Entries close in early March

    AwardUS

    Enter by early March for a shot at exhibition, which is worth more than the cash

  • Wildscreen Panda Awards↗

    Recognition rather than cash across 18 categories of conservation film and media, including a Digital-First category

    Award

    Enter a film in one of eighteen categories, including one for digital-first work

  • Jackson Wild Media Awards↗

    Conservation film and media awards. Recognition rather than cash on the main awards

    Award

    Enter for the recognition and the industry contact rather than for prize money

Nothing in this category matches that.
